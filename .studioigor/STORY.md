## Logline
- A stray cat trapped in a flooded clockwork city must climb and chime the three district belfries before the ancient floodgates lock forever, while searchlight drones sweep the canals below.

## World
- A sunken Venice-like clockmaker city of oxidized copper roofs, giant waterwheels, and rising canal mist; the human builders vanished generations ago, leaving wind-up automatons and automated security drones.
- World rule: sound carries over water — every bell chime echoes to both ancient brass locks and patrol drones.
- Tone: bittersweet mystery (Stray + Studio Ghibli ruin melancholy) · Structure: three acts across 3 districts.
- Truth behind the mystery: the city flooded when the Great Spire's Master Escapement stopped; the stray cat's bell collar is actually the missing escapement pendulum key worn by the last clockmaker's pet.
